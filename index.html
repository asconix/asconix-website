Our products

We build for ourselves too. Kvile is our colour theme for the tools developers work in all day.

Kvile

Colour that lets your eyes rest.

Kvile is Norwegian for rest. It is one calm palette for 52 apps: editors, terminals, shells, command-line tools and browsers, in four variants from night to high contrast.

  • One palette file generates all 52 apps, so a change reaches every one at once.
  • Contrast is measured in every variant, and the accents are checked for colour blindness.
  • A free version and a free 60-page e-book on colour for screens.

How we work

Every project follows the same four steps, whether it is a new website or a full infrastructure rebuild.

  1. 1

    Understand

    We start with your systems, your people and what would hurt most if it stopped.

  2. 2

    Design

    You get a written plan with clear priorities, costs and trade-offs before we build anything.

  3. 3

    Build

    We deliver in small steps you can see and test, and document as we go.

  4. 4

    Run

    We stay on after launch to monitor, patch and improve, or hand over cleanly if you prefer.
